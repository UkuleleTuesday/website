{# youtube_video — renders a click-to-load YouTube embed (a "facade"). Up front only the video's thumbnail (from i.ytimg.com) and a play button are rendered in a 16:9 box. The YouTube player iframe is created by /js/youtube-embed.js when the visitor clicks, on the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain and with autoplay so that the same click starts playback. Without JavaScript the play button is a plain link to the video on YouTube. Pages that use this macro must load /js/youtube-embed.js (see the extra_scripts block in templates/concerts/index.html). Why: a YouTube player pulls in about 1 MB of third-party JavaScript per video and logs a stream of console warnings and errors (Feature Policy, doubleclick, cookies) before anyone has pressed play — see issue #125. Parameters ---------- video_id the YouTube video ID, e.g. "t7B2ez6Uodk" title what the video is; used as the play button's accessible name and as the player's title loading loading attribute for the thumbnail (default: "lazy"); use "eager" for a video that is visible without scrolling #} {% macro youtube_video(video_id, title, loading="lazy") -%}
Play video: {{ title }}
{%- endmacro %}
