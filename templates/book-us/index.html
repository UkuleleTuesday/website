The band: festival and event sets

Our gig group is a compact, high-energy band built around 4–5 ukuleles (with lead lines, riffs and solos), bass, cajon / percussion, and colours like melodica, saxophone and tin whistle, all wrapped in strong lead vocals and rich harmonies. The set brings the best of the high energy bangers played at our jam sessions, leans hard into songs you don’t expect to hear on ukulele — reworked into big, rowdy, full-room singalongs.

For festival and event organisers, we offer flexible 30–60 minute sets tailored to your slot and crowd, from family-friendly daytime shows to late-night chaos. We’re used to professional festival environments, tight changeovers and shared backline: we travel as a self-contained act and typically just need a standard PA with vocal mics, DI inputs for instruments, and monitors.

  • Sets: 30–60 minutes, tailored to your slot
  • On stage: 12 of us, so a stage that fits twelve
  • We need: a PA with vocal mics, DI inputs and monitors. Short on mics, stands, DIs or cables? We can bring our own, just say.
  • Where: Ireland, and festivals abroad
  • Fees and availability: tell us your date, location and setup

If you’re programming a stage and want a crowd-pleasing, high-interaction act that’s a little unusual but instantly accessible, Ukulele Tuesday is a very safe bet. Use the form below to tell us about your event (date, location, setup) and we’ll get back to you about availability and fees.

Weddings and private parties aren’t usually our thing, but if you think we’d be a fit, ask and we’ll tell you straight.

A play-along session for your crowd

Every Tuesday we fill a room with people playing along to songs on a big screen. We can bring the same craic and energy to your festival, community event, company day or school. At festivals we often do it as a combo: a set on the stage, then a play-along for the crowd.

It’s a spectrum. At one end, minimal and jammy: a screen, the band taking turns leading, and whoever turns up with a uke. At the other, the full Tuesday experience, with proper sound and a room singing its head off.

We’ve run this every week for years, and we build our own songbooks and song sheets. For your event we put together a custom songbook from our classics, pitched at your crowd: we adapt the difficulty. Have a look at the one we built for the Ukulele Hooley 2026. We can bring a projector and printed songbooks if you need them, just tell us in advance.

  • Format: anywhere from a minimal jam to the full play-along with sound
  • At festivals: often paired with a band set
  • We bring: the band to lead it, and a songbook built for your event, on screen
  • On request: a projector, printed songbooks
  • Numbers: any size, tell us yours
  • Ukuleles: not provided, people bring their own

Tell us the crowd, the room and the date, and whether you need the projector or songbooks.

Live music for charity and community events

Alongside our big-stage festival sets, we love playing smaller events where live music can make a difference.

Our community gigs include charity fundraisers, Christmas concerts, and special events with partner organisations. They’re acoustic sets with minimal gear, usually shorter and more informal, and tailored to your space and audience.

Community concerts are played by members of the Tuesday session rather than the band: a bigger, looser, all-acoustic group that comes for the fun of it. Expect plenty of singing and a warm, informal set rather than a polished stage show. It’s how our members share the joy of the session with their wider communities, build confidence performing, and give something back.

  • Format: acoustic, minimal gear; shorter, informal sets
  • Who plays: members of the Tuesday session, as many as want to come
  • Cost: usually free, for charities and good causes

If you’re organising a charity fundraiser, community celebration, or local event and think a room full of ukuleles might be a good fit, we’d love to hear from you. Use the form below and tell us more about your event!

Workshops

Between us we have years of performing, jamming, arranging, writing songs and mixing sound, and we can turn any of it into a hands-on workshop. So far we’ve run them as part of festivals we play at, on writing your own songs and on playing comfortably without hurting yourself. We don’t run them outside festivals at the moment. If you’re programming a festival, ask us about adding one to the booking.
