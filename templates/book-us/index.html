Festivals and other gigs

Our gig group is a compact, high-energy band: 4–5 ukuleles with lead lines, riffs and solos, bass, cajon and percussion, and colours like melodica, saxophone and tin whistle, under strong lead vocals and rich harmonies. We play the best of the bangers from our jam sessions and lean hard into songs you don’t expect on a ukulele, reworked into big, rowdy, full-room singalongs.

Sets fit your slot, from family-friendly afternoons to late-night chaos. We’re used to festival stages, tight changeovers and shared backline: we arrive self-contained and need only a standard PA with vocal mics, DI inputs and monitors. Short on mics, stands, DIs or cables? We bring our own.

A play-along session for your crowd

Every Tuesday we fill a room with people playing along to songs on a big screen. We bring the same craic to your festival or event. We are happy to lead a jam session or play-along as part of your festival.

This can be anything from a lightweight jam session to the full Tuesday experience with proper sound, face-melting solos, and a room playing and singing its head off.

We build a songbook for your event from our classics, pitched at your crowd, like the one we made for the Ukulele Hooley 2026.

Live music for charity and community events

We also play smaller events where live music makes a difference: charity fundraisers, Christmas concerts, and special events with partner organisations.

These are played by members of the Tuesday session rather than the band: a bigger, looser, all-acoustic group that comes for the fun of it. Expect plenty of singing and a warm, informal set rather than a polished show. It’s how our members share the session with their communities and give something back.
